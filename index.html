01. About Me

Hello! My name is Tanuj Rana. My journey in technology and academics has been driven by a relentless curiosity and a deep-seated desire to solve complex problems from first principles. Originally hailing from Uttarakhand, where I completed my schooling at Saraswati Vidhya Mandir Inter College Awas Vikas, I developed an early appreciation for analytical thinking.

My academic path took an interesting turn when I pursued a B.Sc. in Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry for a year. This period rigorously trained my mind in linear algebra, discrete mathematics, and abstract logic. However, my true passion lay in building and programming. This realization led me to transition into the B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering program at South Asian University, New Delhi, where I am currently in my first year.

Today, I balance my time between mastering low-level concepts in C programming, developing robust full-stack web applications with PHP, MySQL, and modern JavaScript, and continuously refining my UI/UX design sensibilities. I pride myself on being a multifaceted individual—when I'm not debugging memory leaks or designing glassmorphism interfaces, you can find me writing poetry or playing cricket, finding inspiration and discipline in both art and sport.
